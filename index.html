02 · About

Design meets control

I'm a mechanical engineer doing my master's in Electromobility at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg. I work where mechanical design meets control: in my project thesis at the FAPS institute, I'm developing a lightweight, PID-controlled tape-winding tool for a UR10 robot, from CAD and 3D-printed parts to the sensing and control concept.

Alongside the thesis, I'm teaching myself NVIDIA Isaac Sim by building a KUKA KR210 palletizing cell in Python, one documented step at a time. Before my master's, I designed kaizen improvements that were implemented on an engine assembly line at Toyota Industries in Bengaluru.

I'm looking for a role where I can work on real robotic systems in industry. My German is at A2 level and improving every week.
